Your advocate or your builder. You choose the level of support.

Kino Consulting, Inc. helps clients turn ideas into well-planned, carefully managed projects. We can serve as the owner's advocate, managing planning and coordinating contractors, take on the general contractor role and oversee the construction itself, or lead design and construction together as your design-build team.

Either way, our focus stays the same: clear communication, quality workmanship, responsible budgeting, and accountability.
