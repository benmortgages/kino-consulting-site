Services

Support that fits your project.

From advice on a single decision to full delivery of the build, we help you choose the approach that fits your project, your budget, and how involved you want to be.

Construction Consulting

Experienced guidance on the decisions that shape your project.

Kino's level of responsibility

What It Covers

Guidance on project feasibility, scope, budgets, contractor proposals, and construction decisions. Our involvement can be as focused as advice on one specific decision.

Helpful When You're

  • Deciding whether a project is feasible
  • Setting a realistic scope and budget
  • Comparing contractor proposals or checking them for scope gaps
  • Facing a construction decision and wanting an experienced opinion

Construction Management

We serve as your advocate and coordinate the team on your behalf.

Kino's level of responsibility

What It Covers

We act as your advocate while coordinating design professionals, contractors, schedules, costs, and project progress. You stay in control of the decisions. We keep everything moving and make sure you understand the impact of each one.

What's Included

  • Coordinating architects and design professionals
  • Coordinating contractors and trades
  • Managing schedules and costs
  • Monitoring progress and quality
  • Explaining the cost and schedule impact of changes

A Good Fit When

You want an advocate to guide planning and manage separate contractors.

General Contracting

We take responsibility for delivering the agreed construction scope.

Kino's level of responsibility

What It Covers

When Kino serves as your general contractor, we manage the construction work under one coordinated agreement and take responsibility for quality, schedule, and completion.

What's Included

  • Selecting, hiring, and managing subcontractors
  • Coordinating labor, materials, procurement, and deliveries
  • Overseeing site activities and the sequence of work
  • Addressing field issues through completion
  • Managing quality, schedule, and closeout

A Good Fit When

You want one team responsible for coordinating and delivering the construction work.

Design-Build

One team responsible for both the design and the construction.

Kino's level of responsibility

What It Covers

We take your project from early design through finished construction under one agreement. Layouts, finishes, and budget are developed together, with architects and engineers brought in where drawings are required, so the design is priced and buildable before work begins.

What's Included

  • Design direction for layouts, materials, and finishes
  • Coordinating architects and engineers for drawings and permits
  • Budgeting and pricing as the design develops
  • Selecting, hiring, and managing subcontractors
  • Managing quality, schedule, and closeout

A Good Fit When

You want one team accountable for the design and the build, with a single point of contact from start to finish.

What We Handle

Our capabilities span the full life of a project, from the first scope conversation to the last closeout document.

Before Construction

Planning, design, and budget

Project Planning and Scope Development

We establish goals, priorities, functional needs, and a clear scope of work, and identify potential challenges early so you can make informed decisions before construction begins.

Architect and Design Coordination

We connect your vision with practical construction requirements, evaluating layouts, materials, and finishes with cost, installation, durability, and future maintenance in mind.

Budget Development and Cost Review

We develop budgets, evaluate contractor proposals, identify scope gaps, and review alternatives. When changes arise, we explain their cost and schedule impact.

During Construction

Execution, coordination, and oversight

General Contracting and Construction Execution

As your general contractor, we select and manage subcontractors, coordinate procurement and deliveries, oversee the sequence of work, and address field issues through completion.

Contractor and Trade Coordination

We bring relationships with trusted contractors we've vetted over time, set clear responsibilities, and coordinate trades so their work supports the overall project.

Scheduling and Site Oversight

We organize construction activities, monitor progress, and address issues that could affect delivery, including planning work around occupied spaces and ongoing operations.

Quality Control and Problem Solving

We review work against the agreed scope and requirements, identify concerns, and coordinate corrective work, with attention to both how it looks and how it functions.

At Completion

Closeout and final details

Project Completion and Closeout

We coordinate punch-list items, outstanding work, and applicable closeout documentation so the final details get the same attention as the rest of the project.

Common Questions

If you don't see your question here, ask us. That's what the first conversation is for.

How do I know which service I need?

Start with a conversation. We'll talk through your project, your budget, and how involved you want to be, then recommend consulting, construction management, general contracting, or design-build. Many clients aren't sure at first, and that's completely normal.

Can you help with just one part of a project?

Yes. Our involvement can range from advice on a specific construction decision to full project delivery.

Will you work with my architect or designer?

Yes. We work with architects and design professionals to connect your vision with practical construction requirements, evaluating layouts, materials, and finishes with cost, installation, durability, and maintenance in mind.

Do you have contractors you trust?

We bring relationships with trusted contractors we've worked with and vetted over time. We set clear responsibilities and coordinate trades so their work supports the overall project.

Can work happen while our building stays open?

Often, yes. We help plan work around occupied spaces and ongoing operations, keeping daily operations, maintenance needs, and building occupants in mind.

What happens when something changes mid-project?

We explain the cost and schedule implications of the change so you can decide with a clear understanding of the impact. No surprises, and no unnecessary jargon.

Have a project in mind? Let's talk it through.

Tell us what you're planning. We'll help you understand your options and recommend the right level of support.
