How We Work

From first conversation to final walkthrough.

Every Kino project moves through the same five phases. Here's what happens in each one, what you get, and how involved you'll be.

Five Phases, One Accountable Team

You always know where your project stands, what comes next, and which decisions are yours to make.

  1. 01

    Vision

    We start by listening.

    What Happens

    We talk through your goals, how you'll use the space, your budget, and how involved you want to be. Then we recommend the approach that fits: consulting, construction management, general contracting, or design-build.

    What You Get

    • A clear picture of your goals and priorities
    • An honest early read on feasibility
    • A recommended level of support

    Your Role

    Share your goals, ideas, and any constraints you already know about.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Start with a clear plan.

    What Happens

    We define the scope of work, set a realistic budget, and identify challenges early, before construction begins. Layouts, materials, and finishes are weighed for cost, installation, durability, and future maintenance.

    What You Get

    • A clear scope of work
    • A realistic budget
    • Contractor proposals reviewed for scope gaps

    Your Role

    Make the key decisions on scope, finishes, and budget, with our recommendations in hand.

  3. 03

    Coordinate

    Bring the right people together.

    What Happens

    We bring in architects, design professionals, and trusted contractors we've worked with and vetted over time. We set clear responsibilities and coordinate schedules so everyone's work supports the overall project.

    What You Get

    • The right team for your project
    • Clear responsibilities for every trade
    • A coordinated schedule

    Your Role

    Approve the team and the schedule. We handle the coordination.

  4. 04

    Build

    Stay close to every detail.

    What Happens

    Hands-on site oversight, scheduling, and quality control. We monitor progress, address field issues as they come up, and plan work around occupied spaces and ongoing operations when needed.

    What You Get

    • Clear updates on progress and cost
    • Work checked against the agreed scope
    • The cost and schedule impact of any change, before you decide

    Your Role

    Stay informed and decide on changes. You'll always know the impact first.

  5. 05

    Deliver

    Finish strong. Built to last.

    What Happens

    The punch list and closeout get the same attention as the rest of the project. We coordinate outstanding work and applicable closeout documentation, so your space works well long after construction ends.

    What You Get

    • A completed punch list
    • Closeout documentation, where applicable
    • A space that works the way you planned

    Your Role

    Walk the finished space with us and sign off on the work.
