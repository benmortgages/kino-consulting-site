About

A husband-and-wife team, invested in your project.

Kino Consulting, Inc. is led by Andrea and Jake Aranki. Together they help clients turn ideas into well-planned, carefully managed projects, from early planning through completion.

Flexible support. Personal accountability.

Some clients need an advocate to guide planning and manage separate contractors. Others want a general contractor to take responsibility for the construction work. Kino supports either approach.

Our project management background helps us understand how design, budgets, scheduling, and field execution affect one another. Our trusted contractor relationships connect clients with the right people, and our personal involvement means direct communication and clear accountability.

We also know a successful project has to do more than look good when construction ends. It should function well, suit the client's needs, and provide lasting value.

Andrea Aranki

President and CEO

Andrea brings creativity, organization, and a personal approach to client relationships.

With a background as a classroom and one-on-one special education aide, as well as an experienced administrative assistant, Andrea understands the importance of listening, clear communication, and keeping details organized.

At Kino, she supports client relations, administrative coordination, accounting, and the creative direction of projects. As a designer and visionary, she helps clients explore layouts, finishes, and details that reflect their style and support how they want to use their space. She enjoys helping people see the possibilities and shaping their ideas into a cohesive vision.

Working alongside her husband, Jake, Andrea combines her eye for design with a thoughtful, practical approach. She is committed to helping clients feel heard, informed, and supported throughout the process.

Outside of work, Andrea is a mother of four who brings the same care, creativity, and dedication to her family that she brings to Kino and its clients.

Jake Aranki

Executive Vice President

Jake combines a background in construction project management and facilities leadership with a personal commitment to helping clients build with confidence.

His experience includes serving as a Project Manager with ICI, leading Buildings & Grounds for Addison School District 4, and overseeing Buildings & Grounds operations at Leyden High School District 212. That work spans capital improvements, renovations, building systems, maintenance operations, safety, and contractor coordination, giving him a practical understanding of both how projects are built and how spaces need to function long after construction is complete.

At Kino, Jake brings a hands-on approach built around clear communication, accountability, and client advocacy. He helps clients define their goals, evaluate options, coordinate trusted professionals, and make informed decisions about scope, budget, and quality. Whether serving as a consultant, construction manager, or general contractor, he stays focused on the client's vision and the details needed to bring it to life.

Outside of work, Jake is a husband, father of four, and youth baseball coach. He brings the same commitment to teamwork, dependable leadership, and follow-through to his business that he values in his family and community.

What We Stand For

Whether we serve as your consultant, construction manager, general contractor, or design-build team, these commitments stay the same.

Client Advocacy

We approach decisions with your goals in mind, and work to earn your trust through sound recommendations and responsible project management.

Honesty and Transparency

We communicate clearly about scope, costs, progress, and challenges. We address concerns early and explain options without unnecessary construction jargon.

Accountability

We set clear expectations, follow through on commitments, and take ownership of our responsibilities. When problems arise, we focus on resolving them.

Quality and Long-Term Value

We weigh workmanship, durability, function, and maintenance alongside appearance, helping you invest where it matters and find economical alternatives where they make sense.

Communication and Respect

We respect your time, money, and trust. We provide updates, stay accessible, and keep you involved in the decisions that affect your project.

Strong Relationships

We value dependable contractors, collaborative design professionals, and fair working relationships. They help us coordinate work well and handle challenges constructively.
